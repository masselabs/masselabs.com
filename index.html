Why we build

Trust should require less disclosure.

As identity, finance, and automation move onchain, applications need reliable ways to establish trust. That need should not make collecting sensitive information the default.

We build infrastructure that reduces the information applications need to receive. Our work connects privacy research with the engineering needed to make it useful in everyday products.
