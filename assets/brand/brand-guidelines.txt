Masse Labs — Brand kit
Version: 2026-10-13

Use these assets when referring to Masse Labs. The company name is written
Masse Labs (two words, capital M and L). These assets identify Masse Labs,
not the individual ZKProofport, OpenStoa, or Dojangscan products.

FILES
primary: navy icon and navy wordmark, for light backgrounds.
reverse: white and gold symbol and white wordmark, for dark backgrounds.
stacked: vertical icon and wordmark, for light backgrounds.
icon: original rounded navy tile, for avatars and square placements.
symbol-navy / symbol-white: freestanding two-color symbol.
mono-navy / mono-white: single-color symbol when color is unavailable.
cover: 1600 x 900 brand cover for presentations and social sharing.
SVG files scale without loss. PNG logo files have transparent backgrounds;
the cover has an opaque navy background. White assets require dark backgrounds.
All wordmark lettering is outlined, so no font installation is required.

COLORS
Navy: #0F1E36
Gold: #F5A623
White: #FFFFFF
Paper: #F7F9F5

TYPOGRAPHY
Inter, weight 800 for the wordmark. Inter regular for supporting text.
Inter is licensed under the SIL Open Font License; see INTER-OFL.txt.

USAGE
Keep the proportions, curve, and dot intact. Leave clear space of at least
one gold-dot diameter around the visible logo. Keep the symbol at least
32 px tall on screen and the horizontal lockup at least 160 px wide.
Use the reverse version on dark backgrounds. Do not stretch, rotate,
recolor, add effects, or place on busy backgrounds. Do not imply endorsement
or affiliation without permission.
