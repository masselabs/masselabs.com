05 / Usage

Keep it consistent.

These assets represent Masse Labs, the company. Our products have their own identities.

Download usage guide ↓
  • Give it space. Leave at least one gold-dot diameter of clear space around the visible logo.
  • Keep it readable. Use the symbol at 32 px tall or larger; keep horizontal logos at least 160 px wide.
  • Respect the original. Keep the proportions and colors. Avoid rotation, stretching, shadows, and busy backgrounds.
  • Use the right contrast. Choose the reverse logo or white symbol on dark backgrounds.
